— The House

A garden, a kitchen,
a long table.

Located in the bustling heart of the charming My Khe beach, right next to Nguyen Van Thoai Boulevard. A high-end, independent location — convenient enough for business connections, yet secluded and quiet enough to inspire great ideas.

Casa Mika unfolds across 1,400 square metres of garden, glasshouse and shaded terrace — a table for discerning travellers in the nearby four- and five-star hotels, for affluent locals, and for business guests who want a workspace that finishes with a proper dinner. From the first morning coffee at half-six to the last cocktail before midnight, the garden keeps its own rhythm.

— Atmosphere

Two quiet thefts
of attention.

A wall that breathes. A piano that listens. Built into the architecture of the house, not added to it.

In the main hall

The Fish-Tank Wall

A recessed aquarium running the length of the main dining room — the slow movement of water as ambient theatre, the room's quiet metronome.

From the lobby

A Pianist, Each Evening

A live grand piano in the main hall every night from seven — chosen for the room, not the stage. Conversation stays the loudest sound at the table.
